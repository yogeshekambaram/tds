    How do the group prediction and credits work?

    The group prediction shows what players think. It is an idea, not a fact. New information can change it.

    Credits are practice points. Use some to move the group prediction. You can always save your own answer without using credits.

    Join to see available credits.

    Trading is optional. A share pays 1 credit if its answer wins, or 0 if it loses. Estimated sale proceeds are not guaranteed profit.

    How will I get marks?

    Taking part: 10 marks. How close your predictions are over time: up to 45 marks. Starting early: up to 22.5 marks. Revising your answer when new facts arrive: up to 22.5 marks. Trading credits does not give you marks.
