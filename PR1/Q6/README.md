# PR1 Q6: The Human Exchange (trade services with classmates), up to 10 marks

## The question

In the audio question you recorded a service you can offer classmates. The Human Exchange lists everyone's offers as
text under pseudonyms. Buy services from classmates and sell yours. This game's marks are added on top of the audio
question's marks.

- **Game:** <https://tds-network-games.sanand.workers.dev/trading/human-exchange/>
- **Sign-in:** reload the exam page for a fresh personal link (signs you in for 12 hours; expires if unused after
  20 minutes). Keep it private. It is deliberately not stored here.
- **Exam page:** <https://exam.sanand.workers.dev>

## How a trade works

1. **Buy:** you start with 10 credits. Placing an order holds 1 credit for 48 hours (can be extended once).
   You can have at most **3 open orders**.
2. **Arrange:** chat privately on the order, agree a price of **1–10 credits**, and receive the service outside the
   site. You may swap course emails if you both choose to.
3. **Pay:** enter the price and one sentence saying what you received (needs Google sign-in).
4. **Seller confirms:** the seller enters one sentence saying what they delivered (within 48 hours).
   Only then do credits move and marks count.

## Marks (up to 10)

| Role | 1st new partner | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Selling (new buyers) | 4 | 2 | 1 | 0.5 | 0.25 |
| Buying (new sellers) | 2 | 1 | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.125 |

- Only **seller-confirmed** trades count.
- Repeat trades with the same classmate earn nothing; price and credits earn nothing.
- Unusual patterns are reviewed (e.g. pairs who only trade with each other).

**Best realistic target:** sell to 3–4 different buyers (4 + 2 + 1 + 0.5 = 7.5) and buy from 2–3 different sellers
(2 + 1 + 0.5 = 3.5). That passes the 10-mark cap.
